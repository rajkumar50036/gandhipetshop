## Brand & Style

The aesthetic is themed after futuristic gravity defiance propulsion systems (SpaceX, Apple Vision Pro, Tesla, and high-fidelity sci-fi cockpit control centers).

## Elevation & Depth

- **Base Layer**: Space Black canvas with interactive stars.
- **Glass Layer**: Frosted glassmorphism panels with 24px blur, 1px cyan/purple neon hairline borders, and glowing back-aurora effects.
- **Hover Effects**: Levitation translations, 3D mouse rotation tilts, and glowing cyan dropshadow overlays.
